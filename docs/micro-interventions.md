# 🫁 Somatic Micro-Interventions (Clarity Loop)

This document outlines the selected somatic micro-interventions used within the Clarity Loop system. These interventions are designed to regulate physiological arousal, stabilize attention, and support the transition from cognitive overload to clarity.

---

## 1. Extended Exhale Reset

A guided breathing pattern (e.g., inhale 4, exhale 8) that activates the parasympathetic nervous system and reduces physiological arousal, helping stabilize both body and thought.

---

## 2. Attention Anchor (Bird Tracking)

The user listens for subtle auditory cues (e.g., bird sounds) and tracks their direction or count, gently redirecting attention away from mental overload into present-moment awareness.

---

## 3. Stillness Lock

The user is guided to remain completely still for a short duration (e.g., 5 seconds), interrupting restless motor patterns and signaling safety and settling to the nervous system.

---

## 4. Visual Down-Regulation (Calming Fire Animation)

An animated visual (e.g., a fire gradually calming) provides a symbolic and sensory cue of settling, supporting the user’s nervous system to mirror the transition toward calm.

---

## 5. Haptic Grounding (Body-Localized Vibration)

The device delivers gentle vibrations that the user anchors to a chosen area of the body, reinforcing somatic awareness and helping shift attention from cognitive loops to embodied sensation.

---

## 6. Spatial Audio Anchoring

Sounds are presented with spatial variation (left/right/distance), encouraging the user to orient in space and engage sensory perception rather than internal cognitive noise.

---

## 7. Heartbeat Entrainment

A rhythmic auditory or haptic pulse guides the user to synchronize with a slower, steady rhythm, supporting physiological coherence and calming internal states.

---

## 8. Brightness Breathing

Gradual changes in screen brightness guide inhalation and exhalation, subtly entraining breath without requiring active cognitive effort.
